DATA NOTE

"Appeared Students" is reported for only 1 of 66 incidents (1.5%) in the source dataset — so "students affected" is intentionally excluded as a headline metric here. Treating it as reliable without enrichment from official exam-body records would materially overstate confidence. State names, conducting-body names, and action-taken text were also standardized during cleaning (see scripts/clean_data.py) to correct spelling inconsistencies in the raw source.
